01 · THE LETTER
♡

My dearest Mohid,

Happy birthday to the person who somehow became such a beautiful and important part of my life. I do not think I will ever be able to put everything I feel for you into one letter, but today I want to try. I want you to know how incredibly special you are to me, not only today because it is your birthday, but every ordinary day when you probably do not even realise how much your presence means to me.

There are people we meet and then there are people who slowly become a part of our everyday thoughts. You became the second kind for me. You became the person I want to tell things to, the person I think about when something funny happens, the person I want beside me when a day feels difficult, and the person whose happiness genuinely makes my heart happy.

I love the way you love poetry. Maybe that is one of the reasons I love writing things for you so much. Poetry has always been about trying to explain feelings that ordinary words cannot hold, and somehow loving you feels exactly like that.

It feels bigger than a simple sentence. It feels like a thousand little moments, conversations, smiles, silly arguments, quiet looks, memories and dreams all living together.

And if I could choose one thing for you on your birthday, I would choose a life that is gentle with your heart. I would choose happiness that stays. I would choose dreams that come true one by one. I would choose people who understand your heart and appreciate the person you are.

I would choose success that makes you proud of yourself, peace that makes you sleep without worries, and countless reasons for you to smile.

I hope you always remember that you are capable of so much more than you sometimes believe. I hope you never become so busy chasing the future that you forget to appreciate the person you are becoming.

I hope you keep your softness, your humour, your dreams and that little part of you that makes you uniquely you. And whenever life feels heavy, I hope you remember that you do not have to carry everything alone.

I know I can be annoying sometimes. I know I can overthink, complain, tease you, become dramatic over tiny things and probably test your patience more than I should.

But beneath all of that is a girl who cares about you more than she knows how to explain. A girl who is ridiculously proud to call herself your baby.

A girl who wants to see you happy, even when she cannot personally fix whatever is making your day difficult.

You have become one of those people whose existence feels comforting. Just knowing that you are there can make an ordinary day feel a little warmer.

And I hope I can be that person for you too. I hope I can be your safe place when you need one, your biggest supporter when you are chasing something, the person who reminds you to believe in yourself when you forget, and the person who celebrates even the little victories with you.

I want to make more memories with you. More stupid pictures. More random conversations that somehow turn into hours. More moments where neither of us knows what we are doing but we are laughing anyway.

More days we look back on and say, remember when we did that. More photographs that become little time capsules of us. More stories that belong only to us.

And since you love poetry, here is something I would want you to keep somewhere in your heart.

تیری آنکھوں کی سہولت ہو میسر جس کو

وہ بھلا چاند ستاروں کو کہاں دیکھے گا

روبرو عشق ہو اور عشق بھی تیرے جیسا

پھر کوئی دل کے خسارے کو کہاں دیکھے گا ❤️

Maybe that is exactly how I feel. When something feels this beautiful, everything else becomes a little less important. The world can be full of beautiful things, but there is something about having one person who makes your heart pause for a second.

Mohid, on your birthday, I want you to know that I notice you. I notice the little things. I notice the way you speak, the things you care about, the things that make you excited, the things that annoy you, the moments when you are quiet, and the moments when you are completely yourself.

I hope you never feel like you have to become someone else to deserve love.

You are already enough. You are already worthy of beautiful things. You deserve to be celebrated, appreciated, respected and loved.

And today, I am celebrating you with my whole heart.

Thank you for existing. Thank you for being you. Thank you for all the memories we already have and for all the memories that are still waiting for us somewhere ahead.

I do not know exactly what every tomorrow will look like, but I know that I am grateful for every yesterday that brought you into my life.

So here is to another year of you. Another year of your dreams, your laughter, your poetry, your little habits, your beautiful heart and all the things that make you Mohid.

I hope this year is kinder to you than the last. I hope it surprises you in the best ways. I hope you achieve things you once thought were impossible.

And I hope when you look around on the hard days, you remember that there is someone who believes in you ridiculously much.

I love you in all the ordinary moments too. Not only in the pretty ones. I love the conversations, the chaos, the jokes, the teasing, the random silence and the comfort of simply knowing you are there.

I love being your baby, and I hope I get to keep that title for a very, very long time.

Happy birthday, my love. Please keep smiling. Please keep dreaming. Please keep writing poetry.

And please never forget that somewhere in this world, there is a girl who thinks you are one of the most beautiful chapters life ever gave her.

Always your baby, ♡
